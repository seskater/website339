### Strengths (lead with these)
1. **Published indicative prices** on every product. That is transparent and lowers the barrier to ordering.
2. **Large real-photo galleries** (about 180 cake photos across 4 pages). It is real proof of craft, with no stock imagery.
3. **Broad, clearly organised offer:** cakes for every occasion, minidesserts, koláčky, chlebíčky, jednohubky, Christmas cookies, vánočky, and even Christmas dough for home bakers.
4. **Delivery** ("Dort dovezeme až na požadované místo"), **open 7 days**, an **online order form**, and a promise to reply "během pár hodin".
5. **Personal, authentic story hook:** "Vánoční cukroví z receptů od mé babičky."
6. **Garden** (zahrádka) and café seating.
7. **Hours are consistent** across the website, Google and Firmy.cz.
8. Clean, attractive brand design (logo, colours) that works on mobile.
